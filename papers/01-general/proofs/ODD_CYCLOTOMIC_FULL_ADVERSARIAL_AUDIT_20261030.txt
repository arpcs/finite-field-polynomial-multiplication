Claim audited
-------------
For each fixed odd prime p and fixed e, one deterministic machine with
fixed finite alphabet and fixed finite number of ordinary tapes returns
all ordinary coefficients of f*g over F_(p^e), deg f,deg g<n, in
    O_(p,e)(n (log(n+2))^(9999/10000)).                 (T)
This is the finite-field-theory alternative in the active goal. It is
not a claim about F2, a measured cryptographic speedup, linear workspace,
a published priority record, or a new integer-multiplication bound.

The proof is ODD_CYCLOTOMIC_HOST_TRANSFER_20261030.txt and its included
editor section odd_cyclotomic_host_transfer.tex. Its non-elementary
machine input is the named external stage-two interchange theorem, via
the inspected ordinary-bit wrapper, and the retained native odd-ring
constructions. The formal local compiler, small arithmetic and ordinary
endpoint are DERIVED, rather than supplied as new unproved H/R oracles.

Decision
--------
The new reduction closes its algorithmic, representation and bit-cost
obligations at the contracts itemized below. The result is a mathematical
theorem derivation using explicitly named prior constructions. It is not
obtained by observing PASS reports or by assuming absence of a
counterexample. The actual identities, compiler recurrences and their
cost owners establish each step. An external formalization/build is not
asserted, and the explicit source dependency remains part of the result.
The separate question of worldwide publication priority is NOT established
by the bounded literature comparison below.

Requirements and constructive evidence
---------------------------------------
1. SAME ORDINARY FUNCTION, ALL LENGTHS -- established by derivation.
   Inputs are fixed-p coefficient lists, counted/copied in O_p(n).
   The host satisfies2n<N<4n. Both coefficient CRT components are
   multiplied and reconstructed; the final inverse top map returns
   the cyclic N product. Degree<=2n-2<N makes its first2n-1 entries
   the requested ordinary product, including zeros. Fixed small n
   have an effective direct branch. No sparse, transformed, group
   convolution or quotient output is substituted for this function.

2. HOST AND ROOTS ARE COMPUTED -- established from the named source.
   HvdH3.4 constructs the admissible tuple and auxiliary data;3.8
   constructs alpha, Phi_alpha and the principal N/q0 root. At fixed
   p both costs are N^o(1) in the deterministic fixed-tape model.
   Factor/root data are not length-dependent advice. The published
   source states the implied constants are effective. This is its
   unconditional cyclotomic theorem, not its Linnik-conditional paper.

3. NO PARAMETER CYCLE -- established by fixed topology.
   Fix p,G, the radix ell, formal DAG printer, layer-coloring and
   native permutation generator BEFORE the enlarged coefficient word.
   Retain zero coefficient edges. Thus matching topology and its
   permutation tables do not depend on a computed B coefficient, an
   algebra component, zero/nonunit tests, or D'. The actual per-record
   program is polynomial in R,L. Choose A0 to pay that effective
   polynomial; the helper unit selectors are separate scalar setup.
   This prevents using an unknown D'-dependent polynomial to chooseD'.

4. ENLARGEMENT AND DENSITY -- established by the actual factor count.
   All host primes exceed L0^3, and the original alpha uses O(a^2)
   primes, a=ceil(log2L0), as seen in the proof of HvdH3.8. Appending
   primes until phi(alpha')>=(R0+L0+5)^A0 adds only O(A0*a) factors.
   The available tuple has Omega(L0/a^2) factors. First crossing keeps
   log alpha'=O(a^4), while
       theta=1-phi(alpha')/alpha'
             <=sum_(q|alpha')1/q=O(a^2/L0^3).
   This proves both m'D'=Theta(N) and the small complement invoice.
   A weaker theta<1/L0 would NOT suffice and is not used.

5. ROOT LIFT AND INPUT COEFFICIENTS HAVE DIFFERENT MAPS -- established.
   Primitive alpha'-roots raised to J=alpha'/alpha have order alpha,
   proving Phi_alpha' divides Phi_alpha(U^J). That unital homomorphism
   transports the old root identities. Period splitting cancels the
   factors alpha/q0 and J, each a unit in Fp, to get order m'. The
   NEW coefficient ring is instead identified by Y_new->U. Applying
   exponentJ to new input coefficients would be wrong and is not done.

6. NATIVE SHAPES REALLY FIT -- established by the following extension.
   The relevant earlier native dock/phase statements already allow
   log R bounded by a fixed power of log L. The later parameter lemma
   is worded only for polynomial radices; its statement alone cannot
   be cited at R=2^O((log L)^2). Its PROOF extends as follows:
     h=floor(L/(40ceil(log2R))), t>=(L-2)/log2R>2h;
     the two banks use at most L/20 address bits;
     separate data, tile auxiliary and movement dock leave >=0.8L
     opaque suffix bits for large L.
   This opaque width dominates every fixed polynomial in R,L here.
   The remaining repetition counts are O(log R) endpoint blocks,
   O(log R) ragged slots and O(log t) balanced CRT levels. They are
   fixed powers of log L for this c=2 host. No use of R=poly(L) remains
   except that logarithm absorption, which stays valid. Every outside
   current-record setting computation is paid by the enlarged D'.

7. NATIVE LEADING EXPONENT -- established at the actual supplier boundary.
   The pinned interchange tariff tau_L is below eta_L=1/(2-tau_L).
   The retained source/rest/exit phase induction keeps the exact mixed
   finite word and proves eta_L with fixed secondary logarithms. Its
   plain gamma0=1-619/5000000 is strictly above eta_L. The preceding
   shape extension only adds fixed logarithms, so the native C,
   permutations and coupled CRT retain gamma0 with such factors.
   This imports the primitive interchange component, not the source
   repository's global integer theorem or numerical resampling.

8. SPECIALIZATION AND FIELD LANES -- established by a ring identity.
   The exact finite word has entries in Z[i,1/2]; reducing along its
   homomorphism to fixed G=F_(p^2) is valid for every odd p. Source
   directions, constants and inverse signs are retained. There is no
   map C->G. If i lies in Fp, split G into two fixed Fp lanes and
   specialize the Gaussian residue output at that root; otherwise its
   two-coordinate field code is fixed. These are constant whole-call
   codecs. The result is G-linear on arbitrary supplied lanes, not
   a bitwise interpretation of multiplication modulo odd p.

9. ACTUAL B REPRESENTATION -- established with priced maps.
   B=G[U]/Phi_alpha' is stored as D' consecutive G coefficients. The
   native C therefore acts on genuine independent G lanes. Fixed-degree
   arithmetic may split them into two Fp polynomials on two fixed
   tapes and zip them afterward in O_p(D') scans. No growing-field
   basis conversion, per-axis coefficient extension or free transpose
   occurs. The helper G lanes remain allocated through all transforms
   and point products, then return to zero by the S-valued endpoint.

10. FORMAL CIRCUIT IN ODD CHARACTERISTIC -- established algebraically.
    Choose ell=3 for p!=3, ell=5 for p=3. In
    A[Y]/Phi_(ell*b), all root differences are units by polynomial
    Bezout identities over Fp extended to any commutative A. This
    includes nonreduced algebras. Use inverse normalization t^-1 and
    subtract the final cyclotomic block. The true block products have
    degree<2b<=(ell-1)b and are retained exactly. Overlaps are ADDED
    at reconstruction. The recurrence S(v)<=tS(r)+O(tb logt), with
    r<=v^(2/3) and tr<=80v, contracts at log exponent16 because
    80*(2/3)^16<1. Thus it supplies an actual uniform formal circuit,
    not a unit-cost B multiplication oracle or the target multiplier.

11. LINEARIZATION AND OUTPUT PORTS -- established by formal degree.
    Keep constants and homogeneous degree-one parts with the rule
    (c,l)(d,m)->(cd,cm+dl). Higher degree cannot lower under subsequent
    polynomial operations. Fixed-kernel convolution is homogeneous
    linear, so its desired output is retained exactly. No x^p=x
    identity is used. Count all input/output copy trees; binary fan-in
    and bounded fan-out give g=O(vlog^16v), H=O(log^2v).

12. ARBITRARY DIRTY REPLAY -- established by the exact cancellation.
    On disjoint ports the sweep is z'=Uz+Tx and FT+D=M. The first
    sweep/read/undo adds Mx+FUz; the source-suppressed second read
    SUBTRACTS FUz and is undone. Both source-copy roots and direct
    source output edges are suppressed. This works for arbitrary
    correlated incoming z, over a ring and for nonunit gate weights.
    Matching edge coloring gives O(H) layers. Adding both dirty outputs
    would be incorrect for odd p; the new witnesses detect it.

13. EXACT-WIDTH TOEPLITZ -- established by displacement and counted fit.
    After diagonal children, E=B T_s^-1 satisfies
       E_(i,j)-E_(i-1,j-1)=-h_(i-s)g_(s-j).
    A rectangular chunk has three outer-product displacement terms,
    including explicit first-row/column corrections. Telescoping gives
    its action by SIX fixed-kernel convolutions and the sum. Count all
    intermediate gates/ports. The largest-fit search requires e+a+g<=v
    for every ragged pair, and eventually accepts width Omega(v/log^16v).
    Sources and targets occupy different parent halves; gates come
    from their complement and are restored after every pair. Parallel
    diagonal children yield O(log^35v) disjoint-shear layers on exactly
    v original coordinates. No clean scratch or rounded axis is used.

14. NEWTON SYMMETRY OVER S -- established by unit-pivot factorization.
    Principal q-roots have unit differences in each etale component.
    N=A T diag(beta), beta_j=(-1)^j u^(j(j-1)/2), and N's diagonal is
    unit. Uniqueness of unit-lower/diagonal/unit-upper factorization of
    the symmetric Fourier matrix gives F=N diag(N_jj)^-1 N^t, hence
    F=A T diag(beta_j/H_j) T^t A. The odd sign is not discarded.
    Formal transpose reverses layer order/direction but retains mu;
    no physical coefficient-bit-matrix transpose is asserted.

15. ZERO DIVISORS AND UNIT SPLITTING -- established componentwise.
    S's components have degree r=ord_alpha'(p). B's components all
    have degree lcm(r,2)<=2D'. For lambda in fixed G\{0,1},
       e=1-(mu-1)^(p^rB-1), d=1+(lambda-1)e
    makes d and mu-d units in EVERY component, with mu=d+(mu-d).
    Mu and mu-1 are not assumed invertible. Exponents/inverses take
    polynomial(D') public setup without factoring B. The proof uses
    the fact that fixed G injects into each B component, so lambda
    stays different from0,1 there.

16. GROWING SHEARS USE A FIXED KERNEL -- established by exact words.
    The fixed five-C word produces U(1) over G. For a unit z of B,
    diag(z,1)U(1)diag(z^-1,1)=U(z); two such words give U(mu).
    Thus the number of C calls is fixed, independent of B,n,mu.
    Every growing diagonal is separately charged. This does not
    instantiate a fixed-kernel theorem with a growing coefficient
    field. Zero shears use the same completed identity schedule.

17. SYNCHRONIZATION AND DUMMY STATES -- established by the common template.
    The two Toeplitz halves each have K=max len(T_qi)=O(log^35R0)
    layers. Tensor all axes in each shared slot, inserting identity
    missing layers and three outer diagonals. There is no full-volume
    call per prime. Pair all unused positions, including the one dummy,
    with zero shears. Completed words preserve dummy-pattern subspaces;
    intermediates need not. Keep the entire native grid throughout.

18. LITERAL FREQUENCIES AND NORMALIZATION -- established by CRT characters.
    E_i CRT idempotents give xy=sum_i E_i(x modq_i)(y modq_i) modm'.
    Hence Q^-1 tensor(F_qi(omega^E_i) direct-sum1) Q E=E F_m'(omega).
    This establishes ordinary frequency order, not just a matching
    product roundtrip. The inverse uses inverse roots AND (m')^-1 in
    Fp, so character sums give the true cyclic coefficient product.
    The scalar is not silently set to1 as in characteristic two.

19. BOTH COEFFICIENT CRT COMPONENTS -- established by monic division.
    f=Phi_alpha' and psi=(Y^alpha'-1)/f are coprime because p does not
    divide the squarefree host N. Project every alpha'-coefficient row
    to BOTH factors with prepared reciprocal/monic remainder maps.
    Bezout idempotents reconstruct that row exactly. The possibly short
    psi modulus uses a reciprocal of the actual dividend-minus-modulus
    width, not a falsely short reciprocal. All costs are charged.

20. THE COMPLEMENT FITS -- established using the strong density.
    With d=deg psi, v=dm'=O(N a^2/L0^3), the packing exponent is
    Y^j Z^i->T^(2di+j). Each input has length<=2v and coefficient
    product bands do not overlap adjacent outer slots. Reduce each
    length-2d output band modulo psi and fold Z^m'=1. Its old-word
    cost is O_p(v L0^2 logL0)=O_p(N a^3/L0)=o(N). The d=0 branch is
    omitted and d=1 is direct. No full-input integer packing is used.

21. INDEPENDENT SMALL ARITHMETIC -- established by classical packing.
    Choose k with v(p-1)^2<2^k, multiply the packed integers using
    classical SS, then reduce every exact coefficient chunk modp.
    This costs M_old,p(v)=O_p(v log^2v loglogv) on fixed tapes,
    including encoding/extraction/returns. Fixed monic remainder is
    two such products and scans with a prepared reciprocal. Constant-
    degree G multiplication reduces to constant-many S products.
    No call uses (T) recursively as its own arithmetic premise.

22. SCALAR LISTS HAVE NO AXIS FACTOR -- established by prefix expansion.
    At each current local slot, axis coefficients factor by their
    local native positions. Generate their product list in current
    physical order by prefix expansion. Prefix volumes grow by at
    least2, so their sum is<2M. Table scans/rewinds and all B products
    cost O_p(M M_old(D')). Joint-control CRT scalars use their separate
    fixed-field/current-address generator and are not forced into
    this factorization. Lists are rebuilt after completed relabeling.

23. SETUP AND ARCHIVE ACCESS -- established by fixed polynomials.
    Roots, modulus data, DAGs, edge colors, unit selectors and B-scalar
    tables require polynomial(R0,D',L0) setup=N^o(1). Full sequential
    archive scans are allowed within that same fixed polynomial.
    Active local tables are selected once per axis/slot; no root-size
    archive lookup occurs per payload record. Every variable current-
    address CRT/gauge setting program is paid by D'>=P(R,L).

24. FINITE TAPES, CALL OWNERSHIP AND PADS -- established by composition.
    Use disjoint fixed tape families for host, native, old arithmetic,
    scalar lists and current-record metadata. The parent parks its
    other whole array and complement while the callee owns its current
    array; no descendant scans them. Source current-parent stacks,
    split/rotate/repair returns and field codecs retain their actual
    contracts. Whole-range pads are cropped only at their completed
    boundaries and are not multiplied anew into recursive child volume.
    No tape per point, prime, table or recursion level is introduced.
    No O(n) workspace theorem is asserted; visited space is bounded
    by the stated running time, with linear top-level arrays.

25. FINAL EXPONENT AND ALL LENGTHS -- established by a strict gap.
    The aggregate cost is
       O_p(N L0^gamma0 (log L0)^C)+N polylogL0+N^o(1),
       gamma0=1-619/5000000<9999/10000.
    K=log^35R0 is a fixed power of logL0. All other degrees/constants
    were fixed beforeN. The strictly positive final gap absorbs all
    secondary logarithms with one effective cutoff; finitely many
    smaller inputs use the direct algorithm and a fixed constant.

26. FIXED EXTENSIONS, NO VARIABLE-CHARACTERISTIC CLAIM -- established.
    A fixed polynomial-basis field F_(p^e) is a constant number of Fp
    coefficient lanes. Constant-many prime-field polynomial products,
    then its fixed multiplication/reduction table, give the ordinary
    extension-field result. It need not embed in G or in a host field.
    Constants and cutoff may depend on p,e; the exponent is common.
