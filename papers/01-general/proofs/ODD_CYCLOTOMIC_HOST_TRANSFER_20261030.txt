II. A formal convolution supplier in every fixed characteristic
--------------------------------------------------------------
Fix p odd. Choose ell=3 if p!=3, and ell=5 if p=3. This ell is a
CONSTANT address/root radix; it is not the coefficient characteristic.
For a large input length v, let b be the least ell-power with b^2>=v,
s=ceil(v/b), and t the least ell-power >=2s-1. Then
    sqrt(v)<=b<ell sqrt(v), s<=b,
    t<=ell b, t<2ell s, t divides ell b.
Over ANY commutative Fp-algebra A, use
    R=A[Y]/Phi_(ell b)(Y), rank r=(ell-1)b.
The defining polynomial is sum_(j=0)^(ell-1) Y^(jb). Its roots over
the algebraic closure of Fp have exact order ell b, because ell b
is an ell-power and ell!=p. Thus Y^j-1 is a unit for0<j<ell b:
the coprime-polynomial Bezout identity over Fp remains valid over A.
The root zeta=Y^(ell b/t) is principal of order t, including over
nonreduced A. Its inverse transform has normalization t^-1 in Fp,
not the characteristic-two value1. This scalar is always a unit.

Block the input into s length-b polynomials over A. Two t-point DFTs,
t recursive ordinary length-r products over A, and the inverse DFT
recover all block products. The true blocks have degree <=2b-2<r,
so the quotient loses no true block coefficient. Add overlapping
length-2b blocks and crop only the known padded tail.

Multiplication by a root power pads an r-vector to ell b, rotates, and
subtracts the last length-b block from EACH of the first ell-1 blocks.
For a point product, first fold modulo Y^(ell b)-1 (its degree is
<2ell b), then use that same cyclotomic fold. Both have O_p(b) size
and constant arithmetic depth. The ell-ary butterflies, their explicit
twiddles, normalization and block reconstruction give
    S(v)<=t S(r)+O_p(tb log(2t)),
    D(v)<=D(r)+O_p(log(2t)).
For v above a fixed threshold, r<=v^(2/3), and
    tr < 2ell(ell-1)bs <=4ell(ell-1)v <=80v.
Since80*(2/3)^16<1, induction gives a division-free formal bilinear
circuit of size O_p(v log^16(2v)), depth O_p(log(2v)), with polynomial
wire-descriptor generation. The direct base branch is finite and exact.
Every normalization is a fixed-field scalar, not inversion of data.

Specialize one operand, or retain constants and the degree-one part
at every gate: (c,l)(d,m) -> (cd,cm+dl). No relation x^p=x is used.
Count input/output copy trees. The resulting linear DAG has
    g(v)=O_p(v log^16(2v)), H(v)=O_p(log^2(2v)).
All coefficient constants are prepared once with polynomial work in
the local dimensions and the actual algebra representation.

III. Dirty replay and exact-width Toeplitz: retain the minus signs
----------------------------------------------------------------
On pairwise disjoint source x, target y and borrowed gate coordinates z,
the first in-place linear sweep has z'=Uz+Tx. With counted output ports
Fz+Dx, zero initialization gives FT+D=M. Sweep/read/undo increments y by
    Mx+FUz.
Repeat the sweep with EVERY original-source edge omitted, including
input-copy roots and direct output reads. SUBTRACT its borrowed output
FUz from y, then undo. This yields y+Mx and restores arbitrary dirty z.
Adding both outputs, as in characteristic two, would instead leave
2FUz and is incorrect for odd p. Reverse sweeps use negative shears.
Bounded degree and edge coloring give O_p(H+1) disjoint-shear layers.

For a unit lower Toeplitz matrix T_v, split at s=floor(v/2). Apply its
two diagonal children, then cross-update E=B T_s^-1. If h is the
Toeplitz sequence and g its reciprocal, the absolute-index difference is
    E_(i,j)-E_(i-1,j-1)=-h_(i-s) g_(s-j).
The rank-three displacement and first-row/column corrections in the
existing local appendix hold verbatim over Fp, with these signed
coefficients. Each rectangular chunk action is SIX fixed-kernel
convolutions, including correlation/reversal ports, with both input
chunk lengths <=b and all intermediate/output wires counted.

Choose the largest b for which e+a+g<=v for EVERY ragged chunk pair;
the actual DAG can be printed and counted during setup. Eventually
b=Omega_p(v/log^16v). Sources and targets lie in different half intervals;
all borrowed gates come from their complement INSIDE the same v slots.
Replay restores them before another chunk pair. There are O(log^32v)
chunk pairs and O(log^2v) layers per pair. Diagonal child words run in
parallel, so
    d(v)<=max(d(floor(v/2)),d(ceil(v/2)))+O_p(log^34(2v)),
    d(v)=O_p(log^35(2v)).
No padding of each prime axis or clean gate coordinate is needed.
This is an exact-width compiler, not a unit-cost growing-ring DAG.

IV. Newton Fourier words and arbitrary nonunit shear coefficients
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let S be the etale coefficient algebra below and u a principal q-root,
q a host prime different from p. Put H_j=product_(1<=a<=j)(1-u^a),
H_0=1. All H_j are units in S. The Newton matrix has
    N_(j,k)=H_j H_(j-k)^-1 beta_k,
    beta_k=(-1)^k u^(k(k-1)/2).
The SIGN (-1)^k is essential in odd characteristic. Unit-pivot symmetry
gives F_q=N diag(N_jj)^-1 N^t, over the ring as well as a field. With
A=diag(H_j), T the unit Toeplitz sequence H_j^-1, this is
    F_q=A T Gamma T^t A, Gamma=diag(beta_j/H_j).
The transpose is the field-coordinate matrix transpose, not a physical
transpose of the expanded coefficient-bit map.

Take fixed G=F_(p^2), which contains i^2=-1 and has >5 elements, and
B=S tensor_Fp G, represented as G[U]/Phi_(alpha')(U). Every B component
is F_(p^rB), rB=lcm(ord_(alpha')p,2)<=2D'. It is a product of fields,
not presumed one field. Write lambda for any fixed G\{0,1}. For mu in B,
    e_mu=1-(mu-1)^(p^rB-1),
    d=1+(lambda-1)e_mu.
Componentwise d and mu-d are units, and mu=d+(mu-d). Nonzero-divisor
claims are componentwise; mu itself and mu-1 need not be units.
These selectors/inverses are public setup, polynomial in R0,D',L0.

The existing fixed five-C word gives U(1)=I+E12 over G. Its proof
chooses a in G outside{0,1,-1,i,-i}, forms the upper triangular
C diag(a,1) C^-1 diag(-(a+1)/(a-1),1) C, normalizes its two diagonal
entries, and rescales its nonzero upper-right entry. Therefore for
every UNIT z in the growing B,
    U(z)=diag(z,1) U(1) diag(z^-1,1).
For general mu use U(mu)=U(d)U(mu-d). The number of native C calls and
growing B diagonal slots is an ABSOLUTE CONSTANT depending only on p.
Zero shears receive the same completed identity word. Pair all unused
positions, including the one native dummy, with coefficient zero.
Pair ordering and its inverse are existing generic permutation tensors.

This does NOT invoke a fixed-kernel theorem at a growing field B.
The ONLY accelerated fixed scalar kernel is C over the FIXED G, acting
independently on the D' native G lanes. Growing B diagonals retain the
separate old multiplication tariff. Keep all pair/dummy/G lanes until
each complete word returns. Completed words fix every unused pair and
preserve each per-axis dummy-pattern subspace.

Synchronize the two Toeplitz halves of the five-stage Newton template
separately to K=max_i len(T_qi)=O_p(log^35(2R0)) matching rounds. Missing
layers are identity matchings. Thus there are2K rounds and three outer
tensor diagonals; no whole-array call per prime axis. Every round has
constant-many native C, generic permutation and B diagonal calls.

V. Host, coefficient word, and ALL ordinary coefficients
--------------------------------------------------------
For fixed p, apply HvdH3.4 at target2n, then3.8, to construct the whole
p-admissible tuple and its divisor/root in N^o(1) bit time, with
2n<N<4n. Let L0=ceil(log2N), ell0=ceil(log2L0), R0=2^(ell0^2).
The remaining distinct primes satisfy L0^3<q_i<=R0; none equals p.
The constructed alpha includes q0 and only O(ell0^2) primes.

Freeze the actual polynomial allowance P_(G,family)(R,L), including
controlled CRT and compiled local pair permutations. Its address
generators depend on R,L and fixed topology, not on growing B elements:
retain zero coefficient edges in each public DAG, so no scalar-valued
branch changes its wire topology. Their scalar coefficients belong to
the separate prepared B tables. Choose a fixed effective A0 large
enough that (R0+L0+5)^A0 dominates this allowance at R=R0+1,L<=L0.
Enlarge alpha to alpha'=alpha J using unused host primes until
    D'=phi(alpha') >= (R0+L0+5)^A0.
Only O_p,A0(ell0) primes are added and many remain, since the whole
tuple has Omega(L0/ell0^2) primes. The first crossing gives
    log alpha'=O_p,A0(ell0^4), m'=N/alpha', m'D'=Theta(N),
    theta=1-D'/alpha'=O_p,A0(ell0^2/L0^3).
The last estimate comes from the ACTUAL number and minimum size of
selected prime factors, not just the weaker 1/L0 density bound.

The old-to-new homomorphism Y_old -> U^J is used ONLY to lift roots.
If rho has order N/q0, first take omega=rho^(alpha/q0), then lift and
raise to J. Period splitting cancels the factors alpha/q0 and J because
they are nonzero in Fp. This yields a principal m' root in
    S=Fp[U]/Phi_(alpha')(U).
The INPUT coefficient identification is the rename Y_new -> U,
not a second exponent-J substitution. All root data/certificates and
fixed monic reductions are prepared once in polynomial(R0,D',L0),
which is N^o(1). No root for every input length is assumed as advice.

Use the top isomorphism
    Fp[X]/(X^N-1) -> Fp[Y,Z]/(Y^alpha'-1,Z^m'-1),
    X -> Y^c Z, c=(m')^-1 mod alpha'.
Exponent k=r+m'j goes to (Z-index,Y-index)=(r,cr+j mod alpha'). Both
directions cost O_p(N log alpha') by the established two-factor map.
Project each Y-row to f=Phi_(alpha') and psi=(Y^alpha'-1)/f. Reconstruct
rowwise by Bezout idempotents after both component products. The
new large component is S by renaming its variable. Prepared monic
remainder and CRT reconstruction cost O_p(M_old(alpha')) per row.

Embed the large component in B and pad each remaining prime axis once
from q_i to n_i=q_i+1. The product volume ratio is
    M/m'=product_i(1+1/q_i)=1+O(L0^-2).
L=ceil(log2(2M)) is Theta(L0) and eventually <=L0. The native service
conditions hold with log R=O((log L)^2) and record width proportional
to D', including ordinary coefficient codecs. Keep the same M B words
through both forward transforms, their point product and the inverse.

For CRT idempotents E_i=(m'/q_i)((m'/q_i)^-1 mod q_i), use EXACT local
roots omega^E_i. Let Q be the completed extended prime-residue map.
The tensor T of F_qi(omega^E_i) direct-sum1 gives
    Q^-1 T Q E = E F_m'(omega),
where E is the ordinary-order zero-fill embedding. This is a literal
frequency identity, stronger than a matched roundtrip/product test.
The inverse uses omega^-1 AND normalization (m')^-1 in Fp. This last
factor is generally NOT1. It costs a linear scan. The principal-root
character sums then give the actual cyclic S[Z]/(Z^m'-1) product.
Completed transforms have S entries, hence return S-valued arrays; only
after the inverse remove zero helper G lanes and the dummy complement.

For the complement d=deg psi=alpha'-D', v=dm'=N theta, use ordinary
fixed-p polynomial packing Y^j Z^i -> T^(2di+j) at input length2v.
There is no inner coefficient carry into a neighboring outer slot.
After the full product, reduce each length-2d row modulo psi and fold
Z^m'=1. Its cost is O_p(M_old(v)+m'M_old(d)), with d=1 direct and
d=0 omitted. The strong density makes this o_p(N).

Finally reconstruct both coefficient CRT components rowwise, invert
the top map, and extract the first2n-1 coefficients. Since the original
ordinary product has degree<=2n-2<N, there is no cyclic alias. Thus the
function really is ordinary dense Fp polynomial multiplication, with
ordinary coefficients at BOTH ends. Fixed extensions F_(p^e) follow
by a fixed number of Fp products and their fixed basis/table maps.

VI. Complete bit invoice and finite-tape ownership
---------------------------------------------------
Use the independent classical SS integer algorithm with base2^k packing,
k large enough that v(p-1)^2<2^k, for all small word arithmetic. Reducing
each exact integer convolution chunk modulo fixed p gives
    M_old,p(v)=O_p(v log^2(2v) loglog(4v)).
No call uses the proposed new multiplier as its own premise. Fixed monic
remainder uses reversal, a prepared reciprocal, two such products and
subtraction, valid in every characteristic. Products/reductions in
B=G[U]/f need only a constant number of Fp word products, so each actual
growing-ring scalar action costs O_p(M_old(D')). Unit setup is separate.

Represent a B word as D' consecutive ordinary G coefficients, so the
native C service acts on actual serialized G lanes. Runtime arithmetic
may split each such word into its TWO Fp coefficient polynomials by
one scan to two fixed tapes, compute the fixed-degree extension product,
then zip those two output tapes. Each split/zip costs O_p(D'), including
returns. G's fixed polynomial basis and multiplication table are fixed
once; if i already lies in Fp, no variable isomorphism is introduced.
This constant-column lane conversion is included in M_old(D'); it is
not a free transpose of two growing address fields. The helper G lanes
stay allocated across all full transforms and point products.

At each synchronized tensor diagonal, generate product coefficients by
prefix expansion in CURRENT native order. If M_j are successive prefix
volumes, sum_j M_j<2M. Therefore generation, table rewinds, application
and erasure cost O_p(M M_old(D')), not M times the number of axes.
The controlled CRT diagonals are fixed-field routing scalars with the
separately paid polynomial generator, not these B diagonals.

The local Fourier tensor cost is consequently
    O_p(K MD' L^eta_L polylog L + (K+1) M M_old(D')),
or its already rounded gamma0 version, with K=polylog L0. Include the
constant-many coupled CRT maps per transform. All three transforms,
point products, codecs, root setup, both coefficient components, top
maps and ordinary output have aggregate bound
    O_p(N L0^eta_L (log L0)^C) + N polylog L0 + N^o(1).
Using gamma0 instead is harmless because
    eta_L < gamma0=1-619/5000000 < 9999/10000.
Every fixed secondary logarithm fits the STRICT gap to9999/10000.
All parameter choices and polynomial degrees precede N; effective
constants give one fixed direct cutoff. No input-dependent exponent.

Each imported service includes its actual field codec, preparation,
padding, erasure and head returns. Native pads survive only their
specified completed words; no fresh volume factor is introduced into
