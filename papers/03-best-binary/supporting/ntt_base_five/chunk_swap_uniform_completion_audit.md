## 1. Statement, finite data and dependency direction

There is one deterministic finite-alphabet machine with a fixed finite
number of one-dimensional tapes which, for all positive P,G,B and u>=1,
maps a bit array in lexicographic order on

    [P] x [2^u] x [G] x [2^u] x [B]

by (p,h,g,d,z)->(p,d,g,h,z) in O(V u^tau) steps, with
V=P*G*B*2^(2u) and one fixed tau<1. Its shape processing, workspace,
head returns, padding and unpadding are included. B=1 is permitted.
Ordinary binary shape parameters and a finite alphabet suffice.

Use the fixed rational bit motif with ground set g=48. The explicit
incidence schedule in `../SCALAR_MOTIF_SYMBOLIC_AUDIT.md` exchanges the two
data banks and restores every arbitrary scratch value over F2. The
comparable labels and their rational projections are reconstructed in
`finite_motif_rank_closed_form_audit.md`. Those formulas apply at g=48:
the factor Gram matrix I-J/9 has eigenvalue 1-48/9=-13/3 on its all-one
line, and triple lines have norm two with neighboring lines orthogonal.
All required orthogonal complements are therefore nondegenerate direct
summands. No numerical rank test after reducing modulo a prime is used.

Put v=binom(g,3), z=3*binom(g-3,2), N=v^3, I=3v^2,

    m=g^3=110592,
    W=2N+I(vz+g), L=I*g^2,
    s=W*m-N+2L.

At X sources choose the negative projection -P_U, at other sources zero,
and at gates/sinks the positive projection of the stated label. For every
role w, with rho its scalar endpoint exchange,

    M_out(rho(w))-M_in(w)=I_m.

On comparable ordinary label edges the matrix-difference rank is the
absolute dimension change. The N first X edges instead have 2P_U of rank
one and are included in s. The exact cancelled counts give

    W/N=9633881/1081, L/N=432/1081,
    1-s/(W*m)=217/(9633881*110592).

These formulas and the required strict inequality are checked in
`ternary_role_frame_check.cpp`. Specifically,

    217*2^36 > 12*9633881*110592,
    m<2^17, ln(2)<7/10, hence ln(m)<12.

Using exp(-x)>1-x shows s/W<m^(1-2^-36). Fix
tau=1-2^-36. The weaker exponent 1-2^-50 used by the earlier reduction
also follows immediately. All constants here are fixed, not input lengths.

The bit motif, rational matrices and their factors are finite objects
specified by formulas. They can be generated by terminating finite exact
calculations once, or put in the finite machine description. Such setup
has a fixed time/storage K independent of P,G,B,u. It is counted as O(K),
which is absorbed by a fixed constant in O(V u^tau). No input-dependent
advice, uncomputed variable irreducible polynomial or prime oracle appears.

Choose a fixed odd prime q avoiding every denominator in the finite matrix
table and every diagonal-unit numerator/denominator in its lower-triangular
rank factorizations and inverses. Finite exact elimination and trial division
find one. The same q works for every b in Z/(q^b). Fixed radix q and the
fixed role/tape counts, however large, satisfy the model's finite constants.

## 2. Power widths: exact framed role program

First work with two chunks of e=m^k radix-q digits and an independent
designated row field R divisible by W^k. That row field precedes both
chunks, but arbitrary spectators may occur before/after it and between the
chunks. In particular an interval of spectators between the row field
and the first target is retained; it is not absorbed into or divided with
the row count. One fixed-size descriptor can list
[prefix][row][pretarget spectators][target][gap][target][suffix].

For k=0, e=1, interchange two fixed-radix digits by the split/merge
construction of `fixed_tape_primitive_reconstruction.md`, O(V).

For k>0 divide each chunk into m consecutive fields of b=e/m digits,
writing their coordinate vectors H,D in Z/(q^b)^m. To obtain
H<-H+D, split whole rows cyclically into W role streams within each prefix
before R. Stream w receives rows r=W*a+w. Every role contains the complete
H,D ranges and spectators, and exactly R/W rows: its volume is V/W and
its row count is divisible by W^(k-1).

For a fixed rational matrix M define the array permutation

    Phi_M : (H,D)->(H+M*D,D).

It is bijective for every M, including singular M; its inverse is Phi_-M,
and Phi_A Phi_B=Phi_(A+B). Specialize the fixed rational matrices modulo
q^b. On each circuit edge from vertex i to j apply Phi_(M_j-M_i) to its
role stream, then execute each XOR gate pointwise on its aligned arrays.

For clarity, if the physical input on role w is f_w, define its logical
interpretation as Phi_(-M_in(w))*f_w. This is a proof invariant, not an
unperformed preprocessing operation required by the algorithm. An edge
changes the stored frame to the common next gate frame; that common
permutation commutes with pointwise XOR. The finite scalar network routes
the logical input from w to rho(w). Therefore its physical output there is

    Phi_(M_out(rho(w))-M_in(w))*f_w = Phi_I*f_w.

Merge output role rho(w) back into original row class w. This undoes the
role exchange and applies H<-H+D to every original row, scratch rows
included. It preserves the designated row value and every spectator.

To implement an edge matrix A of rational rank a, factor A=E1*Pi*E2 as
in the primitive reconstruction. Lower-triangular transformations cost
O(V/W). The a pivots of Pi require a swaps of b-digit H/D fields, and
each of those uses the inductive power-width algorithm at k-1 with
the same designated row field now divided by W. Its other fields become
spectators, including pretarget fields. The induction therefore applies
to every pivot pair, not just the first or adjacent pair.

There are exactly s recursive swaps across all edges. All other edge
arithmetic, scalar gates and row split/merge work cost C*V, with C fixed.
An arbitrary pointwise gate may touch many roles, but its fixed number
of aligned XOR scans changes C only. It does not change the child count.

Finally turn that one earlier-target shear into the full chunk exchange:

    D<-D-H; H<-H+D; D<-H-D,

componentwise in the m fields. Only the middle operation uses the finite
network. The two outside operations target later fields controlled by
earlier fields and cost O(V), including negative unit scaling. On (h,d)
the result is (d,h). Thus no factor three multiplies s in the recurrence.

## 3. Time, storage, descriptors and parent returns

With time divided by current logical volume, the recurrence is

    F_0<=C0, F_k<=(s/W)*F_(k-1)+C1.

The same fixed work tapes are reused at every depth. Inactive current role
streams are parked on a stack, the active V/W stream is passed to the
child, and current parent work tapes are cleared. On return the parked
streams are popped from their right ends and written right-to-left into
prepared destinations. Those are the linear copies/returns proved in
`fixed_tape_primitive_reconstruction.md`. There are s child calls, but s
is fixed. Their parent parking cost is C_s V, the additive term above;
there is no multiplication of child time by W, s or recursion depth.
Descendants never traverse a parked ancestor. Erasure is over the visited
current interval only, not an ancestor's previous maximum tape extent.

At depth j the child volume is V_j=V_0/W^j. Both original complete chunk
ranges remain as target or spectator fields, so V_j>=q^(2e0), where
e0=m^k was the root width. Also j<=k=O(log e0). Hence

    log V_0 = log V_j + j log W = O(log(2V_j)).

A fixed polynomial of the root descriptor bitlength fits O(V_j) at that
child, even for one-bit suffixes. Building a local descriptor needs only
the current fixed m+m fields and collapsed spectator intervals, not all
ancestor subdivisions. Counts, prime-coefficient preparations, canonical
lengths and stack instructions therefore fit the charged work uniformly.
The retained ranges, rather than free metadata access, justify this bound.

Let a=s/W<m^tau. The geometric solution of the displayed recurrence is
F_k=O(m^(k*tau))=O(e^tau); if a<=1 the at-most-linear-in-k sum also fits
that bound. Physical parked storage is counted in time and is never
substituted for the child's logical volume V/W. Along one active branch
the parked volumes sum geometrically, O(V_0), plus fixed tables and the
smaller descriptors.

## 4. Supply rows from the original high digits

For arbitrary q-digit width e let k=ceil(log_m e), and choose the least
integer rho>=0 such that q^(2rho)>=W^k. Both choices are computable by
ordinary integer multiplication/comparison, without a logarithm oracle.
For sufficiently large e, 1<=rho<e and rho=O(log(2e)); all other e form
a fixed finite base range and use e single-digit exchanges. That base
range has a computable fixed threshold since W,m,q are fixed and
rho<=C(log_2(e)+1) for an explicit fixed C.

Write the high/low fields as h+,h-,d+,d-, each high field having rho digits.
Exchange the corresponding high digits individually and move h+ next
to d+, giving the exact field orders (outer prefix/suffix suppressed):

    (h+,h-,gap,d+,d-)
      -> (d+,h+,h-,gap,d-).

This costs O(V*rho) by elementary digit moves. The joined field (d+,h+)
is a preceding row field with R=q^(2rho)>=W^k. Pad its range, separately
within each earlier prefix, to

    R'=W^k*ceil(R/W^k), R<=R'<2R.

Writing e-rho in base m partitions both remaining chunks into matching
consecutive pieces, with at most m-1 pieces of width m^j at each scale.
Apply the power-width interchange to each corresponding pair. All calls
use the one padded row field R', divisible by every required W^j.
Completed calls preserve its row coordinate, so padding stays zero.
It is added once and cropped once, not multiplied into every recursion.
The total cost is

    O(V)*sum_j a_j*m^(j*tau) = O(V*e^tau), 0<=a_j<m.

The remaining field order is (d+,h+,d-,gap,h-). Crop to R genuine rows,
then move h+ after the gap, giving (d+,d-,gap,h+,h-), the desired swap.
That movement costs O(V log(2e))=O(V e^tau). Every piece and high-field
move preserves all external spectators. Computing the O(log e) piece
schedule and canonical shapes costs polynomial(log(2V)); the two full
q^e ranges make its total O(V), including shape preparation/cleanup.

The final core is never invoked on a width rounded *up* by adding target
digits. Only existing high digits supply rows, and only the independent
row range receives a less-than-two padding factor. The coefficient e^tau
therefore measures the original width, not a polynomially larger one.

## 5. Binary chunks by numerical range padding

For binary width u choose the least e with Q=q^e>=2^u=N. Then Q<q*N.
Pad each chunk's numerical range from N to Q. For each valid first chunk
value and each gap value, copy its N valid second-chunk blocks and append
Q-N zero blocks; append whole zero fibers for invalid first values.
These are fixed-depth nested scans, with all lengths prepared, and cost
O(V') where V'=P*G*B*Q^2<q^2 V.

Numerical radix-q lexicographic order is integer order, just as binary
lexicographic order is. No per-record base conversion or random scatter
is performed. Apply the preceding q-power swap. Its final permutation
preserves the set h<N,d<N, so the invalid entries are zero again.
Crop them by the same nested range scans in output order. Cropping is
done only after the entire q-power operation; partial piece swaps need
not preserve this numerical validity predicate.

Since e=O(u) for fixed q, the resulting cost is O(V u^tau), uniformly in
P,G,B, including B=1. The less-than-q^2 numeric padding is outside the
whole q-power algorithm; the less-than-two row padding is outside its
piece loop. Both are removed before this primitive returns to a caller.
Thus repeated callers, such as H2 role nodes, receive arrays of their
original volume and do not inherit either padding factor in child mass.

## 6. Composing recursive families without rescanning outer data

Use distinct fixed sets of tapes for the finite number of subroutine
families: shrinking coset transforms and their batch stacks; native field
arithmetic; the H2/ternary role recursion; the SWAP recursion; and the small
classical integer multiplier. Each family recursively reuses its own fixed
work tapes and stacks. Their sum is still a fixed finite number of tapes.

For example, a SWAP called inside a ternary address shear copies only that
local array into its own I/O area. The ternary parent's other role streams
remain on its separate tapes, with their heads fixed. Likewise an H2 Taylor
call on one coset row does not park or scan every unprocessed coset row.
Copying the local input/output costs its own volume and is already inside
the corresponding bound. No growing number of tape heads is attached to
coordinates or recursive levels. This explicit family separation prevents
an outer-parent copy from being charged for every internal primitive step.

The dependency graph is acyclic apart from strictly decreasing local
recursions: SWAP uses smaller-width SWAP and bit XOR; H2 uses smaller-
selected-width ternary calls and SWAP; Pascal/radix uses H2 and SWAP;
coset transforms use smaller-field transforms and independent native
scalars; the outer product uses those transforms and an established
classical small integer multiplier. SWAP does not call H2, field polynomial
multiplication, or the sought global product algorithm.

## 7. Directional rectangle correction and bounded evidence

The rectangle tail elsewhere had ambiguous stable-partition wording.
Sorting by the short coordinate only transposes when that coordinate
was on the right. Replace it in both directions by elementary digit
moves: move a right-hand short field's bits first-to-last to the front,
or a left-hand short field's bits last-to-first to after the long field.
Each route costs O(V*min(a,b)), preserves the short field's bit order,
and has an explicit reverse route. The Euclidean-cutoff rectangle bound
O(V(a+b)^(1/(2-tau))) is unchanged.

Its cutoff need not use real arithmetic. For fixed rational
gamma=1/(2-tau), take L=ceil(log_2(a+b)) and
t=2^ceil(gamma*L). Integer multiplication/division computes the exponent;
t is within a constant factor of (a+b)^gamma. The same bound follows.
Native component split/join uses these opposite-direction bit moves.
The outer T-by-q matrix transpose pads T to its next binary power by
less than two, moves its O(log T) bits in the right direction, and crops
the unchanged invalid coordinate. Its O(n log T) cost is justified in
both directions, not by sorting an already leading field.

`chunk_swap_lift_check.cpp` checks the lift around explicitly labelled
ideal power-width permutation oracles. It passed 72 binary cases with
294,840 unique original values, two further multi-piece q-power cases
with 81,920 values, and all padded zero checks. There were 62 internally
padded-row cases and 64 piece calls. It also executes actual split/merge
single-digit moves in both directions on 150 rectangles, checking
69,192 values and inverse routes; 60 short-leading negative controls
expose the old naive orientation shortcut. Its largest array is 97,200
32-bit values; self-only 64 MiB and 10 CPU-second caps were installed.
The compiled C++ run passed in about one second.

These tests verify the finite surrounding permutations and repair, not
the astronomical actual motif or asymptotic bit cost. The latter follows
from the independently reconstructed finite identities, exact rank
inequality, elementary tape implementations and recurrence above.

## 8. Audit conclusion and global scope

The explicit finite bit motif plus ordinary tape constructions establish
SWAP in the stated model; an unverified global integer multiplication
algorithm is not a premise of this local proof. This closes the last
named machine interface of `h2_from_chunk_swap_integrated_audit.md`.

The resulting chain supplies a *proposed complete proof* of the original
F2 multiplication target when combined with the ordinary coset/boundary
ledger. This local conclusion does not establish complete verification:
the next required audit covers the whole construction's uniformity, ordinary
input/output, setups, shrinking-array batch descriptors and endpoint
packing as one algorithm. The directional tape repair must be retained
in that audit. Small PASS reports alone are insufficient for completion,
and no claim of mathematical novelty is made here.
