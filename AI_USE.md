# AI assistance

This work was developed with substantial generative AI assistance in
mathematical exploration, proof development, writing, coding, and literature
review. AI tools also assisted with partial Lean formalization. No AI system is
listed as an author.

## Tools, roles and review provenance

The release-preparation and source-inspection work in this project used
OpenAI Codex. Earlier exploration involved AI assistance as described above;
this release does not contain a complete historical model/version log.
Specific model identities, compute totals and private prompts are not
reconstructed from incomplete records.

The papers include a bibliographic reference identifying the known Codex
software: Nicolas Bonamy and Derrick Choi, *Codex as a platform: build on
the open agent harness*, OpenAI Developers, 19 August 2026
([official reference](https://developers.openai.com/blog/codex-as-a-platform)).
This is a software-use acknowledgment, distinct from citations to OpenAI's
mathematical preprints. The latter are credited for the mathematical
statements and constructions actually used.

Some frozen internal review notes use the label “Luna.” Those notes do not
identify a complete versioned model record. The label is retained as
historical provenance and does not establish a model-specific citation
or independent human review.

First-person language in historical supporting notes records the originating
inspection, not a statement that the named human author performed it. Current
paper status files identify AI-assisted inspection explicitly.

Lean verification is not yet complete; full formalization is planned for a
later update. Supporting Lean work in the broader project is not included or
represented as a proof of these complete multiplication theorems. See [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) for the exact evidence scope.

Private conversation exports are not included. The manuscripts, source files,
version records, and verification receipts provide the release evidence, with
their scope and limitations documented in this package.

New contributions should name the AI tool and model/version when known,
describe its role, and state what the contributor personally checked. Mark
unavailable details as unrecorded. Private conversation exports are not
required for contributing; precise statements, sources and reproducible
artifacts remain the review targets.
