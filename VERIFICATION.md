# Verification scope

These are written research preprints open for mathematical review. No independent human
mathematical review, peer review, complete theorem-level Lean proof, accepted
race record or exhaustive novelty clearance is claimed.

| Evidence | Scope |
|---|---|
| Written proof and focused source inspection | The stated construction and documented internal AI-assisted checks of its interfaces; not independent human certification of every step |
| Historical bounded C++ receipts | The finite instances and identities in the reports; not execution of the complete asymptotic machine |
| Fresh bounded C++ replay of the odd-branch finite unit, 31 October 2026 | Exact fixed scalar endpoint, Laurent controller, direct geometry and invoice/moment checks; source/input hashes, commands and fresh outputs are recorded in [verification/finite-unit-replay.json](verification/finite-unit-replay.json) |
| PDF builds, reference scans and hashes | Document/source association and package integrity; not proof correctness |
| Supporting Lean work in the wider project | Exact checked components at their recorded snapshots; none is represented as a proof of these complete multipliers |

The selected controlled-CRT paper supplies its kernel, routing and arithmetic
contracts in the written construction or cites the precise prior result.
The selected odd-field proof includes its native source and exact stage-two
boundary. No independent rebuild of those external proof closures was run.
A cited prior theorem and a new open interface are different dependencies.

The odd-branch finite-unit replay completed at 01:48:05 UTC on 31 October
2026. It checked the 22,803-event native word on all 770 public scalar input
columns and 799 outputs, the base and forward 32-column Laurent controller,
the 104-port phase/basis geometry, and the exact 66,834-paid/13,444-target
profiles and rational moment bounds. All three checkers passed and matched
their historical expected reports; the runner also matched every paid/target
bin in the pinned public RESULT. The [finite-unit bundle](papers/01-general/proofs/finite-unit/README.md)
provides the complete small checker/input closure and immutable upstream
provenance; [fresh text outputs](verification/finite-unit/) are separate from
the unchanged historical expected reports. Compilation was serialized under
a bounded Windows launcher; each checker installed its 64 MiB/10 CPU-second
limits and had a 30-second wall limit.

This is an internal AI-assisted finite replay. The native frame operators
and all-column lowering, chronological tensor construction, uniform
fixed-tape costs, rounding and complete multiplication theorem still depend
on the written mathematical argument and its cited results. The replay is
neither an execution of the full asymptotic algorithm nor independent human
or Lean proof certification.

Research branches with explicit unclosed uniform Pascal and full-index
packing hypotheses are excluded from this first release. Their stronger
exponents are not advertised as established results here.

Full Lean formalization is unfinished and planned for later. No complete
Lean proof of the selected fast multiplication theorems is supplied.
Lean sources and theorem-verification receipts are not included in this release.

Read the paper STATUS files and [REVIEWING.md](REVIEWING.md). Source and copy
hashes identify exact versions; a historical audit of a different hash does
not certify a newer file. The simple binary witness reports are historical
receipts, not fresh checks performed for this publication pass.
